Book catalogue

Every book gets an inventory number automatically. Search books by title, ISBN, author or reader, group them by author or inventory state and find the archived ones.

Issue books and never miss an anniversary

Librarians issue books to readers, library administrators write them off. The chatter keeps the history of each book, and once a month the module plans an activity for the books with a publication anniversary.
